ClearWalls — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 July 2026

ClearWalls ("the app") is a wallpaper application published by Rahul Daswani ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Data we collect

  • Usage analytics (Google Firebase Analytics): app opens, screen views, and feature interaction events, tied to a pseudonymous app-instance ID. Used to understand which features are used and improve the app.
  • Crash diagnostics (Google Firebase Crashlytics): stack traces, device model, OS version, and app version when the app crashes. Used to fix bugs.
  • Advertising data (Google AdMob): device advertising ID, coarse ad-interaction data, and, where you consent, personalised-ads signals. Used to serve and measure ads, which fund the free app. You can change your ads consent any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options, and reset your advertising ID in Android system settings.
  • AI generation prompts: text prompts you type in the AI tab are sent to Pollinations.ai to generate the image. Prompts are not linked to your identity by us. Do not include personal information in prompts.
  • Content reports: if you report a wallpaper, we receive the content ID, your report reason, and the app version — no account or identity.

Data we do not collect

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, precise location, photos, or files. Favorites and settings are stored only on your device.

Third parties

Data listed above is processed by Google (Firebase, AdMob) under Google's privacy policy, and by Pollinations.ai for AI image generation. We do not sell personal data.

Consent (EEA/UK and applicable regions)

Where required, the app shows a consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform) before any advertising data is processed. You can withdraw or change consent in Settings → Privacy.

Data retention and deletion

Analytics and crash data are retained per Firebase's standard retention periods. Uninstalling the app stops all collection; on-device data is removed with the app. For any privacy request, contact us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. AI generation is filtered for unsafe content and all catalog images are reviewed before publication.
